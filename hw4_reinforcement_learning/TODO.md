### What is the difference between policy iteration and value iteration in terms of their update procedures?
    - Policy iteration updates value based on the actions the policy chooses and has 2 alternating phases, while Value iteration chooses the max Q value at every step and does not alternate phases

### What happens if the discount factor gamma is close to 0 or 1?
    - if the discount factor is close to 0, the policy becomes short sighted, only considering short term rewards. If the discount factor is close to 1, the policy instead prioritizes long term rewards.

### How does increasing the slip probability (slip_chance) affect the optimal policy?
    - as we increase slip probability the policy becomes more risk adverse, propogating negative values further away from negative reward zones, oe in our case the cliff.

### Compare the cases slip_chance = 0.0, 0.01, and 0.2. Why does the agent tend to behave more conservatively as stochasticity increases?
    - as randomness increases, the agent has a higher chance of accidentally falling off the cliff, since it doesnt always follow the policy. This means the expected value of states close to the cliff are considered riskier.
